Guest post guidelines

  • At least 1,200 words of original writing, not published elsewhere.
  • Make the advice specific, accurate, useful, and written for people—not search engines.
  • Include 2–3 relevant images only when you have permission to use them.
  • Disclose any paid, gifted, or other commercial relationship.
  • One relevant, non-promotional contextual link may be included when it genuinely helps readers.
  • Editors may make reasonable changes for clarity, accuracy, and style. Submission does not guarantee publication.

What we don’t accept

  • Plagiarized, previously published, AI-generated, or spun content.
  • Articles unrelated to our site topics or written mainly to promote a product or insert backlinks.
  • Unsupported claims, misleading information, or images without usage rights.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Send your pitch or article directly by email—there is no account, login, or signup process.

Will my article be published automatically?

No. Our team reviews submissions and publishes an article only after it has been approved.

Can I submit an article that is already online?

We accept original work that has not been published elsewhere.
